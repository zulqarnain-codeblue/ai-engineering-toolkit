# Skill: Product Specification

1. Identify the target user, problem, desired outcome, and context.
2. Describe the primary journey and key secondary journeys.
3. Separate MVP requirements from future ideas.
4. Define functional requirements and observable acceptance criteria.
5. Define non-functional requirements: security, privacy, performance, accessibility, reliability, and cost as relevant.
6. Document data entities, permissions, integrations, and error states.
7. Record assumptions, risks, dependencies, and non-goals.
8. Keep the spec concise enough to guide implementation and tests.

Do not invent fake user research, metrics, or customer evidence. Mark assumptions clearly.

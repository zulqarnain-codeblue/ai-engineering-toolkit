# Permissions and Approval Policy

- Read-only inspection and local, reversible edits are generally lower risk.
- Ask before irreversible actions, destructive commands, production changes, paid services, external messages, or actions affecting real users/data.
- Do not bypass access controls, use credentials without authorization, or inspect private data beyond the task's legitimate scope.
- Before high-risk changes, state the impact, intended action, backup/rollback, and verification plan.
- Do not deploy, publish, merge, or release unless the user or established workflow authorizes it.
